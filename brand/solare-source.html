The line runs in three stages.

Clean technology built by LME specifically to process end-of-life PV, operating in Europe for twelve years before it reached Queensland.

Stage 01

Deframing

A mechatronic machine separates the aluminium frame, junction box and cables from the panel. Nothing is cut, pried or hammered, so the laminate reaches the next stage undamaged.

Stage 02

Automated disassembly

Robotics complete the disassembly and split the laminate into its constituent materials. No operator handles a panel, which is what keeps the streams clean and the site safe.

Stage 03

Materials processing

Each recovered material is processed, separated and automatically packed, ready to re-enter a different industry sector. Chain of custody records follow every pallet.

Six things this plant does not do.

Most PV recycling is a demolition process wearing a green label. The recovery rate is decided by what you refuse to use.

01Manual dismantling
02Shredding
03Crushing
04Pyrolysis
05Thermal treatment
06Hydrometallurgy

Every one of those methods mixes the streams it is trying to recover. Shred a panel and the silicon ends up in the glass. Burn it and the polymers become an air-quality problem. Separation is slower to engineer and it is the only route to 99+%.
