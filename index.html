02 / Our materialsNothing lost. Everything possible.

A better future.
Already in the panel.

A solar panel is a collection of valuable resources. Our process separates each layer so it can return to productive use.

01

Silicon

Si

The heart of every solar cell. Recovered from the laminate as a dedicated stream, preserving the material for its next application.

Output / Wafer fragments
02

Aluminium

Al

The frame is removed before the laminate is processed. Separated aluminium can be baled and returned to the metals industry.

Output / Furnace-ready metal
03

Copper

Cu

Busbar ribbons, junction box tails and cable are separated during disassembly, recovering the conductive metal for downstream processing.

Output / Sorted copper
04

Glass

SiO₂

The front glass is separated from the cell and polymer layers, creating a clean material stream for use in other industries.

Output / Recovered glass
05

Plastic

Poly

Backsheet and encapsulant polymers are separated from the other materials and packed for downstream processors.

Output / Separated polymers
03 / Our technologyIn partnership with LME

Precision separation.
Extraordinary recovery.

Engineered to separate a panel into its constituent materials. No shredding. No pyrolysis. Just a smarter way to recover what matters.

Stage 01 /

Deframing

Mechatronic equipment separates the frame, junction box and cables, preparing the laminate for recovery.

Stage 02 /

Automated disassembly

Robotics separate the laminate into its constituent materials, keeping each stream distinct.

Stage 03 /

Materials processing

Recovered materials are processed and packed to re-enter industry, with chain of custody records.
