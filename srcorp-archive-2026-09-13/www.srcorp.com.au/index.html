Welcome

Solar Recovery Corporation is committed to diverting all end-of-life solar PV panels from landfill streams and to the recovery of all the materials from decommissioned panels for use in new applications to create new local jobs in new industries and delivering positive outcomes for our environment, sustainability practice and human health.

End-of-life solar PV panels leave behind toxic waste

Solar PV Panels are an increasingly important source of renewable power in Australia that will play an essential role in fighting climate change. However, at end-of-life the solar PV panels become a source of toxic waste.

Currently, Australia does not have a circular economy solution for end-of- life solar PV panels.

Standard e-waste recycling methods are not suitable for decommissioned solar PV panels. Recovering the materials from end-of-life solar PV panels requires a specific and specialised recycling solution.

Laws & Regulations

SRC will drive and maintain a benchmark standard for the management of end-of-life (EOL) Solar PV Panels across Australia, within a circular economy framework.

Victoria has banned solar panels from landfill with other states to follow. SRC has a single focus of diverting all Solar PV Panels from landfill for processing and maximum material recovery. 

On 1 July 2021 new laws commenced under the Environmental Protection Authority (EPA) Act 2017. There is a strong focus on General Environmental Duty (GED), which requires businesses to be responsible for protecting the environment and human health. 

There is a requirement for a chain of custody records  for reporting the flow of end-of-life Solar PV Panels from the business site, to a lawful place for processing and the destination for recovered materials. 

SRC has developed an end-of-life solar PV panel complete management policy.

The SRC management policy aligns with: 

a) All regulatory requirements 

b) All legal requirements 

c) The EU WEEE Directive 2012 / 2019 

d) 2018 National waste Policy; Less Waste More Resource

e) Victorian Circular Economy Policy 2020

f) Waste Management Policy e-waste 2019

g) EPA Guidelines for storing, transport and processing of e-waste 2021
